How many conversions did the ads actually add?

Criteo ran randomized advertising holdouts and released a sample of 13.98 million user records. I analyzed it, checked the headline numbers with a second implementation in SQL, and compared targeting models on held-out users. A count of conversions among users with a recorded ad exposure was 23,031; the experiment estimates about 13,687 additional conversions, so the count is 68% higher.

2 · Without an experiment

Without an experiment, the credit depends on the attribution rule.

The simplest rule credits the ads with every conversion by a user who was shown one. The experiment estimates how many conversions the ads added; the comparison is at group level and does not identify individual conversions.

3 · Planning a test

Measuring the effect again requires a test, and a large one for conversions.
