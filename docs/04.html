Does the new-customer metric count returning buyers, and who is least likely to return?

Two years of transactions of a UK online retailer (794,163 lines, 5,875 accounts). The metric tables are rebuilt in SQL and match the Python results in all 92 cells.

04

Does the "new customer" metric count returning buyers as new, and which high-value customers are least likely to return?

Of the 1,036 accounts this retailer would label new under a 3-month look-back, 640 (62%) had bought in the prior 12 months, so acquisition metrics here use a 12-month look-back. Among high-value accounts, a purchase-model score ranks who buys again within six months better than recency alone (AUC 0.79 against 0.75). For ranking sales over the next 12 months, a simple past-sales rule did better in this holdout.
